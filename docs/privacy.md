# MailBust Privacy Policy (DRAFT - for review)

_Last updated: 2026-10-21. This is a plain-language draft; review and adjust before
publishing, and have a lawyer check it if MailBust handles traffic at scale._

MailBust provides temporary, disposable email inboxes. We tried to collect as little
as possible.

## What MailBust receives

- **Incoming email** sent to `@mailbust.com` addresses: the sender, subject, body,
  and any attachments. We extract a likely verification code/link for convenience.
- **Basic request data** needed to run the service (e.g. your IP address, used only
  for rate limiting and abuse prevention - not sold or shared).

MailBust is **receive-only**. It cannot and does not send email on your behalf.

## How long we keep it

- Messages and attachments live only until the mailbox **expires** (15 minutes by
  default, 24 hours maximum), then they are **permanently deleted** automatically.
- We do not keep backups of expired mailboxes.

## Public vs private mailboxes

- **Named mailboxes** (e.g. `testing@mailbust.com`) are **public**: anyone who opens
  the same name can read their mail. Never send anything sensitive to a named mailbox.
- **Random mailboxes** are **private** and readable only with the secret token issued
  when they are created.

## What we don't do

- We don't require an account, name, or payment.
- We don't sell or share your data with advertisers.
- We don't use incoming email content for anything beyond showing it to you and
  extracting the code/link.

## Abuse & contact

Report abuse or privacy concerns to **abuse@mailbust.com**.

Because MailBust is disposable and some mailboxes are public, do not use it for
anything you need to keep private, secure, or permanent.
